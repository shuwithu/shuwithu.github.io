• The Arborist

    Digital Art

  • Topic
    Interactive Installation; Biometrics XR

  • Team
    Shu Wei, Barnabas Lee, Michael Gancz, Kimberly Hieftje, Asher Marks

The Arborist is an interactive mixed reality installation that transforms physiological data into living digital art.

Using real-time signals such as heart rate and skin conductance, each participant grows a unique digital flower. Its colour, movement, and form respond to their internal rhythms.

As more people contribute, these individual blooms accumulate into a shared ecosystem: a collective tree shaped by the traces of those who came before.

Rather than visualising raw biometric data directly, the work translates it into a poetic and abstract form, framing sensing as a medium for reflection, co-creation, and collective presence.
